# Twofold — Claude Design Brief

Sep 30, 2026 · @Sono Technologies

Twofold (working title) is a couples-only mobile app: a private world for two with chat, a shared avatar home and pet, memories, streaks, and a couple rank they can show off on WhatsApp, Instagram and TikTok. Paste this brief into Claude Design to create the brand and the key mobile screens.

## Goal

Design Twofold so it feels like a private, playful world for two. It should feel warmer and more personal than WhatsApp, and fun enough that couples want to show it off on social media.

## Brand personality

- Warm, playful, intimate, a little cheeky
- Modern and premium, not cheesy: no clip-art hearts or cupids
- Culturally at home in Nigeria but appealing worldwide

## Visual direction

- **Colors:** a deep plum or wine base, a warm coral or rose accent, soft cream backgrounds, and a gold highlight reserved for ranks and Premium. Provide a full dark mode.
- **Typography:** a rounded, friendly display font for headings and scores, with a clean sans-serif for chat and body text.
- **Shapes:** soft rounded corners, pill buttons, bubbly chat bubbles.
- **Motion:** small delightful moments, such as the streak flame flickering, the pet reacting when fed, confetti on rank-ups, and a heartbeat pulse when a partner comes online.
- **Avatars:** stylized 2D characters with diverse skin tones, hairstyles (including braids, locs, afros, cornrows, headwraps) and outfits, including Nigerian styles like ankara and agbada.

## Screens to design (mobile, 390px wide)

1. **Onboarding:** welcome, sign up with phone number, create your avatar.
2. **Pair up:** invite your partner with a code or link, waiting state, "You're linked" celebration.
3. **Home (Our Space):** both avatars in the shared room, pet, streak flame, bond score and rank, partner's mood, today's daily question.
4. **Chat:** messages, photos, voice notes, stickers, virtual gifts, and a "soften this" AI button near the send button.
5. **Daily question:** answer, locked partner answer, reveal when both have answered.
6. **Pet and room:** feed and play with the pet, decorate the room, item shop.
7. **Memories:** timeline of photos and milestones, anniversary countdown.
8. **Location:** map with both partners, clear "You're sharing" banner, pause and share-for-1-hour controls.
9. **Ranks and badges:** current rank, progress to the next rank, badge collection. Ranks: New Flame, Sweethearts, Ride or Die, Power Couple, Soulmates.
10. **Couple friends leaderboard:** friend couples ranked by score, showing only avatars, rank and streak.
11. **Premium paywall:** Free vs Premium comparison, monthly and yearly plans, "one plan for both of you".
12. **Settings and breakup:** privacy controls, and a calm, respectful "End relationship" flow with a clear confirm step.

## Share cards (most important for growth)

Design shareable cards in 9:16 (Stories and Status) and 1:1 (feed) sizes:

- **Couple Card:** both avatars, names, rank, streak flame with day count, days together.
- **Rank-up card:** "We just became Ride or Die" style announcement.
- **Milestone badge card:** 100-day streak, anniversary.
- **Monthly Wrapped:** 3 to 5 swipeable story slides summarizing the month.

Every card carries the Twofold logo and a small "Get Twofold" line. Cards should look good enough to post on their own. Design standard and Premium (exclusive, animated-look) versions.

## Tone for copy

Short, sweet and a little playful. Examples: "Day 42 together on Twofold", "Your pet misses you both", "Don't break the flame." The breakup screen stays neutral and kind, never jokey.

## Deliverables

- Logo and app icon
- Color, type and component system (buttons, chat bubbles, cards, badges, rank chips)
- The 12 screens above in light and dark mode
- The share card set
- Avatar and pet style sheet
